# Order to Cash (O2C) Finance Operations

## Overview

This module demonstrates an end-to-end Order-to-Cash (O2C) process using a practical finance operations model.

The workflow covers the complete cycle from customer and sales order management through billing, cash collection, accounts receivable ageing, and management reporting.

## Process Areas Covered

### 1. Customer Master
Maintaining customer information including:

- Customer ID
- Customer Name
- Customer Type
- Payment Terms
- Credit Limit
- Customer Status

### 2. Sales Order Management
Recording and monitoring customer sales orders, including:

- Sales Order ID
- Order Date
- Customer
- Product / Service
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Order Value
- Order Status

### 3. Billing & Invoicing
Managing customer invoices and ensuring accurate and timely billing.

Key activities include:

- Invoice creation
- Invoice validation
- Due-date calculation
- Payment-term validation
- Invoice status monitoring
- Outstanding balance calculation

### 4. Cash Application
Recording customer payments and applying receipts against the appropriate invoices.

Activities include:

- Payment identification
- Invoice matching
- Receipt posting
- Unapplied cash monitoring
- Outstanding balance reconciliation

### 5. Collections Management
Monitoring outstanding customer balances and supporting collection activities.

Key areas include:

- Overdue invoice tracking
- Customer follow-up
- Collection status
- Payment recovery
- Escalation of overdue accounts

### 6. Accounts Receivable Ageing
Analysing outstanding receivables based on ageing buckets.

Ageing categories include:

- Not Yet Due
- 0–30 Days
- 31–60 Days
- 61–90 Days
- 90+ Days

### 7. O2C Dashboard
The dashboard provides management-level visibility into:

- Total Sales Orders
- Total Invoiced
- Total Collections
- Outstanding Accounts Receivable
- Collection Rate
- Ageing Position
- Collection Performance

## Key O2C KPIs

### Collection Rate

Collection Rate measures the proportion of invoiced amounts that have been collected.

**Formula:**

Collection Rate = Total Collections / Total Invoiced

### Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)

DSO measures the average number of days required to collect receivables.

**Formula:**

DSO = Average Accounts Receivable / Credit Sales × Number of Days

### Outstanding Receivables

Outstanding Receivables represent invoice amounts that remain unpaid after considering customer receipts.

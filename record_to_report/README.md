# Record to Report (R2R) Finance Operations

## Overview

This module demonstrates an end-to-end Record-to-Report (R2R) finance process using an Excel-based accounting model.

The workflow covers the complete cycle from chart of accounts and journal entries through general ledger processing, trial balance preparation, month-end close, and management reporting.

## R2R Process Flow

Chart of Accounts
↓
Journal Entries
↓
General Ledger
↓
Trial Balance
↓
Account Reconciliation
↓
Month-End Close
↓
Financial Reporting
↓
Management Review

## Process Areas Covered

### 1. Chart of Accounts

Maintaining the accounting structure used to classify financial transactions.

### 2. Journal Entries

Recording financial transactions using appropriate debit and credit accounts.

### 3. General Ledger

Posting journal entries to individual ledger accounts and calculating account balances.

### 4. Trial Balance

Preparing a trial balance to verify that total debits equal total credits.

### 5. Month-End Close

Performing accounting close activities including:

- Journal entry review
- Account reconciliation
- Accrual review
- Depreciation
- Balance validation
- Trial balance review
- Management review

### 6. Financial Reporting

Using validated accounting information to support financial reporting and management analysis.
